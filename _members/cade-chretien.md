Cade Chretien is currently working towards his M.S. in Cyber Security and Privacy at the University of Central Florida, where he previously received his B.S. in Computer Engineering in August 2025. His research interests span a wide variety of areas, including computer networks and security, automotive electronics, Internet of Things (IoT) devices, and system integrations. Outside of academia, Cade enjoys boating, hiking, traveling, and spending time with his family and friends.

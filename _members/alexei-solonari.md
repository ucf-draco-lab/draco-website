Alexei Solonari is a senior undergraduate student in Computer Engineering on the Comprehensive track. He has a strong interest in embedded systems and constrained environments, currently leading the AEGIS project for side-channel analysis of embedded machine learning inference. Alexei has also contributed to the lab's CHIRP project for decentralized IoT communication, and has prior research experience in improving configuration-based Linux kernel testing. Outside of academic work, he enjoys spending time with friends, collecting music, and exploring older consumer technology.

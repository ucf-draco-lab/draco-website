Sebastian Garayua is a third year Computer Engineering student at the University of Central Florida with interests in embedded systems, computer architecture, PCB design, and hardware integration. Their project experience includes Blue Origin sponsored Project Kestrel, where they supported UAV propulsion and airframe integration and designed a test bench mount enabling controlled motor testing. They also designed a battery voltage monitoring PCB with KiCad, including schematic, layout, and component selection with power regulation and microcontroller driven ADC sensing. Sebastian serves as a project co-lead for an IEEE Gaming CPU project and leads the MMU team, providing documentation, pseudocode, and integration support with the CPU architecture. Their current focus is expanding into ultra-lightweight IoT communication research through the projects at DRACO lab.

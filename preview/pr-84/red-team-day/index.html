Run Red Team Day.

Twenty-six 8th and 9th graders are visiting from Wales school for one afternoon of breaking things on purpose. Four pods, eight crews named for UCF mascots, nine lockboxes, and zero laptops. We need 6 to 10 DRACO students to run it.

# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-screwdriver-wrench" %}Senior Design

DRACO sponsors senior design and capstone teams. The ideas below are scoped for two semesters of work by an ECE or CS team, with faculty oversight and lab hardware behind them — real, contained problems that produce something you can demo, not paper exercises.

These are distinct from our [research projects]({{ site.baseurl }}/projects), which take on students as research assistants over a longer horizon. If you are looking to join the lab rather than to run a capstone, start there.

Several of these ideas have been attempted before. That is deliberate: the prior teams' work is documented, and the version number tells you how many runs an idea has had. Read what came before, then beat it.

## {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-lightbulb" %}Bring Your Own

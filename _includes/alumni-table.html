{%- comment -%} Tabular view of lab alumni, one row per degree awarded. Someone who earned both a B.S. and an M.S. with the lab appears twice — once per degree — so that grouping by level and sorting by year of award both stay meaningful. Rows carry data-* attributes; _scripts/alumni-table.js reads those to filter, multi-sort and group without touching the DOM text. filters same "field: regex" string data_filter takes elsewhere, e.g. "role: (ms-alumni|^alumni$|phd-alumni)" Degrees come from a member's `degrees:` frontmatter: degrees: - level: B.S. # B.S. / M.S. / Ph.D. major: Computer Engineering year: 2026 A member with no `degrees:` block is skipped — nothing to put in a row. Every Liquid tag below trims its own whitespace. This include is called from a markdown page, and kramdown ends a raw-HTML block at the first blank line — leave the assigns untrimmed and the tail of each row gets escaped into literal "</a>" text.
